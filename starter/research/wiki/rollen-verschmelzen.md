# Roles are blending: builders instead of silos

## Current synthesis

People at Anthropic describe development, product and design blending together, while individuals still keep specialties. Alex carries this over to QA: builders with a quality specialty. The testing community pushes back that a tool cannot carry responsibility. Ismail Amla gives this a picture: before AI you hired T-shaped people (broad, one deep specialty); with AI people become O-shaped (round, their strongest strength changes depending on the problem).

## Claims and evidence

- In some places the title software engineer is being replaced by “builder”. Boris Cherny, Lenny's Podcast 43:06. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- In the Claude Code team every role codes. Cherny 41:25. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- Roughly 50 % overlap between roles, some people have specialties. Cherny 42:46. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- “Our roles are blending together”. Cat Wu, Anthropic article. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- A human should still look at the code. Cherny 17:13. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- T-shaped = broad knowledge plus deep expertise in one area. O-shaped = round and flexible, “whose strongest contribution may change depending on the problem, the tools available, and the organization around them”. Amla. [raw](../raw/2026-08-12-o-shaped-people.md)
- AI provides “technical scaffolding” so people can apply judgment they already have across disciplinary boundaries. But it does not create taste or domain knowledge. Amla. [raw](../raw/2026-08-12-o-shaped-people.md)
- “Specialists remain essential, but they increasingly guide, review, and strengthen work that begins outside their departments.” Amla. [raw](../raw/2026-08-12-o-shaped-people.md)

## Tensions and open questions

- James Bach: an AI tool cannot be responsible, so it cannot carry a responsible process. At the same time he expects testers to become “generalist thinkers”. [raw](../raw/rollen-verschmelzen-notiz.md)
- Neither Cherny nor Wu mentions QA explicitly. Carrying it over to QA is Alex's own thesis.
- Tension with Alex's slide “Everyone has a specialty”: Amla stresses that the strength changes and is not fixed. At the same time he says specialists become reviewers. That fits QA as a review and judgment role. [raw](../raw/2026-08-12-o-shaped-people.md)
- In front of a QA audience, “everyone can do everything” can sound like a threat. So stress the specialty and the encouraging turn.
